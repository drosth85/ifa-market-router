MOBILKI GSM01 · GRADING STANDARD
Grading standard

One scale.
No surprises.

Cosmetic grades match functional performance — a fleet buyer's grade means the same thing every time.

GradeCosmetic conditionFunction & battery
A+
as new
 No visible marks. Indistinguishable from new under normal use. Fully functional. Battery health ≥ 90%.
A
excellent
 Very light micro-marks, invisible at arm's length. No screen damage. Fully functional. Battery health ≥ 88%.
B
good
 Light visible signs of use — minor scratches, no cracks. Fully functional. Battery health ≥ 85%.
C
fair
 Clearly used — deeper scratches / marks, screen intact and clear. Fully functional. Battery health ≥ 80%.
Grade thresholds shown are the proposed Mobilki standard — align final battery-health cut-offs and cosmetic wording with your actual QC before print. New (sealed) units are sold as Brand New, separate from the refurbished grades above.
