MONSTELO.04 · BUYING FROM US
Commercial terms

Why buying from us pays

Four reasons resellers keep coming back. Prices, MOQ and payment terms are agreed per account — ask at the stand.

Top brandsThe brands that actually sell. Consumer electronics, audio, phones and accessories — 241 brands in rotation, ~11k active EANs.
Where it comes fromWe buy surplus, so you buy sharp. Overstock, clearance, customer returns, open-box and end-of-line — bought from suppliers across Europe and priced against live market data. That is where the margin in your basket comes from.
Best pricesBest brands, best prices. On top brands we negotiate down to 50% of wholesale. Ask for the current list — prices move with the market.
Sourcing networkTell us what you need — we will find it. If it is not in our stock, it is in our network. Between us and our partners we source practically any product. Bring us a hard one.
Buying volumeBulk pricing without the bulk commitment. We collect demand from the whole market and buy in very large quantities, so you get sharp prices against your MOQ instead of financing a container yourself.
Logistics~80% of orders out within 24h, ~35% same day. B2B across 28 EU markets, shipped from one warehouse in Poland.
